Ravara — terms of use

Ravara is a private, non-commercial home dashboard built and used by its owner for their own household.

Who can use it

Only the owner, for vehicles and devices they own or are authorised to use. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

It reads status information (such as battery level, fuel level, mileage and lock status) from the owner's own vehicles through the manufacturer's official APIs, to display on the owner's dashboard. It does not sell, share or publish this information.

Commands

It is used read-only and does not send commands to vehicles.

No warranty

It is provided as-is for personal use.
